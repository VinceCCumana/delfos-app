DELFOS privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

DELFOS is a personal research tool used only by its owner.

Google user data

  • DELFOS requests only the drive.file permission. It can see and change only the files it created itself in its owner's Google Drive. It cannot read any other file in that Drive.
  • It uses that permission solely to store and verify its own files.
  • It does not collect, sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other people

DELFOS has no other users and collects no personal data from visitors to this page.
